# Classroom pilot record

**Created:** 2026-09-08 · **Last-updated:** 2026-09-08 · **Status:** DRAFT observation instrument v1

No student observations have been entered by the author. The operator reported distributing the first notebook for thirty minutes of independent work; that report does not supply individual completion times or outcomes.

Use anonymous local learner codes. Do not collect names, credentials, private prompts or business data. Keep raw attempts in the instructor’s local class folder; share anonymized summaries only when authorized. A blank field means unobserved, not failure or success.

## Before class

Record notebook filename and SHA-256, cohort commit, Python version, environment, planned duration, actual start time and whether the first notebook was the thirty-minute core or the full ninety-minute lesson. Restart the kernel and rehearse the exact distributed artifact. Keep an untouched copy and a separate answer key.

Save predictions before execution. Save the first attempted function/source even if wrong. Collect stdout/stderr, case status, broken/repaired observations, a causal trace and the learner’s explanation. Distinguish student, teacher and automated runs. Capture incomplete work and interruptions instead of dropping them from the denominator.

## Instructor synthesis after observation

Report enrolled, present, attempted, completed without help, completed with help, and incomplete counts separately. Report median/range of measured completion times only for the explicitly named denominator; retain missing times as missing. List common setup errors and misconceptions with anonymized examples. Compare novel transfer reasoning with the original attempt before claiming improvement. Do not interpret a small uncontrolled pilot as causal evidence that this teaching design outperforms alternatives.

## Readiness decision

Apply the guide’s rubric to actual work. Record keep/revise decisions for pacing, prerequisites, examples and assessment cases, with evidence. An externally reviewed manuscript and green executable checks remain distinct from observed learning, an approved human render, or publisher acceptance.
