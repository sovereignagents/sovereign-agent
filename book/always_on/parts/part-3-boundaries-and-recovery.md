# Let it act with boundaries and recovery

**Created:** 2026-09-07 · **Last-updated:** 2026-09-07 · **Status:** DRAFT

Lucy permits an exact purchase, the supplier accepts it, and the reply disappears. Meanwhile a worker may die or return after its replacement has started. A supplier document may contain instructions that no operator authorized. One optimistic success flag cannot resolve all of these situations.

You separate permission to act from evidence of the external effect, and both from ownership of the work. Exact-proposal approval, stable operation identifiers, reconciliation, leases and fenced writes each answer a distinct question. The supplier remains controlled teaching infrastructure, so you can inspect the outcome independently and reproduce a lost reply without making a real purchase.

The final chapter in this part introduces a small MCP client and a narrowly restricted code tool. Model instructions remain useful guidance; filesystem, network, credential and lifetime controls require enforcement outside generated prose. The recorded container failure and repair give that distinction a concrete test.
